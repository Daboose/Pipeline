Sales Campaigns · Mockup: one members list for Campaign Management and Pipeline (invented data, nothing built)

About this mockup

Is this the unified view you meant? One members screen, reached from two places. Switch Opened from below to see both.

  1. Opened from Campaign Management: the screen you already know (Edit campaign, Deactivate, Delete campaign, add a surgeon, Import members, Remove campaign members), now with the deal and rating columns.
  2. Opened from Pipeline: the same list, with a link back to Pipeline instead of the management tools, and a small Link a deal button on surgeons with no deal yet.

The columns I propose (every column sorts, resizes and keeps its heading locked, like the other tables): Surgeon, Current Hospitals, Country · City, Type, Rating (the surgeon's rating for the campaign's portfolio), then the deal or deals: Stage, Revenue / Year, Close Quarter, Last Posted, Last Stage Move, Sales Rep. A surgeon with two deals gets a second line under their name. Tiles at the top count surgeons, those with a deal, those with none, and the deals; the value is shown per currency, never added together.

Worst case: press Campaign covers: All four portfolios (top bar) to see a campaign across Hip, Knee, Extremities (Shoulder) and CMFT: one Rating column per portfolio, plus a Portfolio column telling you which portfolio each deal is in. One portfolio is the ordinary case.

Try it: filter All / With a deal / No deal yet, type in the search box, tick a surgeon and press Remove (it asks first), switch Dark and Light.

Not drawn: the graphs of the approved Campaign view (ratings with and without a deal, stage counts, countries) and the Campaign and Pipeline toggle; they sit above this list in the Pipeline version, as in docs/mockups/archive/pipeline_campaign_view_v1.html. ROSA deals are not in campaigns.
