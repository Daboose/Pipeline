PIPELINE Pipeline Sales Campaigns
ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

What it shows: the Rating column of a Pipeline list, drawn four ways, side by side, at the size the real list uses, with the same invented rows in each. A is what the list has today (a flat colour, as wide as the word, so the plates are different widths). B keeps the flat colour but gives every plate the same width, wide enough for PLATINUM. C uses the brushed metal of the big plates, small, one width. D is C with the tiny star coin and the screws, as on the big plates. Invented data only.

Try it:
  • Compare the four boxes: the Rating column is the third one.
  • Press Light or Dark to see both themes.
  • Press Full screen (top right) for the whole screen.
Not shown yet: the rest of the list, the hover effect (the big plates have a sheen that sweeps across), and the Campaign list.
