PIPELINE MOCKUP v2: MANAGER SCREEN

ABOUT THIS MOCKUP
The manager web screen: choose which deals to look at (the data selectors), then see the totals, the stages, the quarters, each rep, and the deals, with the 30 and 90 day warning colours. All names, teams, deals and numbers are invented (the brand and segment names are the real ones). Nothing is built in Salesforce.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V1
  • Change selection now drops a panel from the very top that covers the whole screen, and the screen behind is dimmed and blurred. Only the panel can be used until you press Apply (keep your choices) or Cancel (go back to how it was). Opportunities | Risks and Open | Closed | Both are now inside the panel too.
  • Brands now follow the segment, as they do on the record types today. Pick Knee and you get the Knee brands (Persona, Nexgen, Oxford...); pick Hip and you get G7, Taperloc, Avenir...; Extremities has only Comprehensive and Other; CMFT has Sternalock, Pectus and RibFix. With no segment picked you see every opportunity brand (25); Risks can involve any brand (39). Choosing a segment drops any brand that no longer fits.
TRY IT
  • Change selection, then pick Knee and look at the Brand list, then Hip, then Extremities. Try Risks.
  • Cog (top left): settings flyout with Dark | Light (works now) and your saved filters (star = opens by default; Share gives the other person their own copy).
  • Full screen (top right) drops the Salesforce bar (in the real tool it opens a new full-screen tab).
  • Pick the team Southeast Asia (sample), or go manual: Singapore + Malaysia + Thailand, Extremities. Pick Australia and New Zealand together to see separate currencies.
  • The Needs attention toggle (the "stale" filter; wording is switchable below) shows only deals past 30 or 90 days. Deals on hold are never flagged. Click a rep to focus on them; click a column heading to sort.
NOT SHOWN YET
Editing a deal, the Chatter feed, the splash screen, the mobile rep screen, the surgeon summary, usages and med ed events. Brand lists here use the Australian record types; the real tool would use each country's own. Colours (mint = current, orange = needs update, red = very old) are proposals.
