ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. In Pipeline's deal panel, the Yes / No fields (On hold, Rep priority, State priority and MyMo) are today a small toggle button. Robert chose B (label inside the pill). He asked for them in the same one-pill slider style as the stage path and the Campaigns list, but plain: no gradient, just No and Yes. This page shows today beside two ways to do it, so you can choose.

  • Today: a toggle button that says No or Yes.
  • A. Label above: the field label sits above the slider, like every other field in the panel, and the slider sits in the grid.
  • B. Label inside the pill: the label is the left end of the slider (“ON HOLD | No Yes”), the same as “In campaign” in the Campaigns list, and the four sit together in a row of their own, so the label is not written twice.
  • MyMo stage (Robert, 2026-11-07): it is hidden while MyMo is No and folds open when MyMo is Yes, so there is nothing to look at that does not apply. Flick MyMo to see it.
  • Try it: press No or Yes on any slider. The highlight slides. Deal changes which fields exist (an opportunity has all four, a risk and a ROSA deal have the two priorities). Can edit shows the greyed, untouchable version for a person who cannot change the deal.

Not shown: saving (the panel's own Save and Cancel are unchanged; the sliders change the draft, as the toggle does now).
