PIPELINE Pipeline Opportunities & Risks
ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. Adding a new opportunity, risk or ROSA deal from the phone. Until now the phone only lists and edits the rep's deals; this adds the third thing, creating one.

  • Phone 1, how a rep starts (four ways; the switch above the phones changes it). D (Robert's idea, shown first) is a bar locked at the bottom of the list, one filled pill saying “+ Create new pipeline”, like the “+ Add usage” button on the Usages screen; it takes you straight to the New deal page (phone 2) and the page lets you choose the kind. It costs the list a strip about 66 px high at the bottom of the screen. The earlier three: A a full-width “+ New deal” button at the top of the list, B a round “+” button floating at the bottom right, C a round “+” on each section heading (Opportunities, Risks, ROSA), which already says what kind it is.
  • Phone 2, the New deal page (it slides in; its own “‹ Back to My Pipeline” bar and a locked Cancel / Create bar). A slider picks the kind (Opportunity, Risk, ROSA); for an opportunity a dropdown picks the portfolio (Hip, Knee, Extremities, CMFT). Salesforce decides the record type from the person's country and that choice, as with a new usage: a rep in Australia gets AU HIP, AU KNEE, AU RISK, AU ROSA and so on, and only the ones their profile allows are offered. Under that are the same fields as editing that kind of deal (try the slider), the stage starting at the first one with the stage list to change it (a deal sometimes starts further on), and the lists of brands, currencies and the rest read from Salesforce for that record type. The owner is the signed-in person. Try it: press the kind slider, tap the stage, tap a person or hospital, press Create.
  • Phone 3, when something is missing: required fields are marked * (Primary HCP, the quarter and the value on an opportunity or a risk; the hospital on a ROSA deal); pressing Create with one missing shows what to fill in, and Salesforce's own refusals (a quarter in the past, a lookup that does not allow that person) show in the same place, nothing is lost.
  • Phone 4, when it works: the new deal opens straight on its deal screen (it slides in) with “Created.”, and the list is read again so it is there when you go back.

Not shown: the person and hospital search pages (the same sliding search pages as when editing), and ROSA's three platform stages (the same as editing a ROSA deal). The desktop Pipeline has no new-deal button; this would be phone only unless you say otherwise. Nothing new on the server is expected: it would create the record with Salesforce's own record service as the signed-in person, so field access, validation rules and the existing automations all run as they do on the standard page. No new object, no new field.
