ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

What it shows: one deal, four screens. The top row stays on every screen: the deal's name, the four pills and Previous, Next and close. The first pill says what kind of deal it is (for example Extremities Opportunity, Knee Risk or ROSA); the lit pill is the screen you are on. Each screen takes the whole space under the top row, instead of a small panel opening inside the deal. The Usages screen is the real Usage Card from the Account page (the metal rating plates, then the usages by hospital with every field of each portfolio) and it scrolls inside the panel while the top row stays put. Invented data only.

Try it:
  • Press the four pills in the top row. On Opportunity the facts row, stage path and the deal's fields show; on the other three they are gone.
  • On Surgeon Usages press the pencil on a usage to edit every field, press Add usage to add one, and scroll the panel: the top row stays where it is. Make the browser window shorter to see how it behaves on a small laptop.
  • Under Deal choose Extremities opportunity, Knee risk or ROSA to see the first pill and the other pills change (ROSA has no Surgeon Usages, as today; its MyCase Bookings are the hospital's).
  • Press Light or Dark; press Full screen for the whole screen.
Not shown yet: Delete, the stage slider, Chatter posting, the slide-in motion and the blurred page behind. The Usage Card's own MyCase table is left out of the Usages screen here, because MyCase Bookings is now its own screen.
