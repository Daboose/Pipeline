PIPELINE Pipeline Opportunities & Risks Accounts
ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 3, invented data only. A sales rep puts one deal at a time into one or many campaigns, from the standard Salesforce record page of the deal, on a desktop or on the Salesforce phone app. Robert chose the Campaigns button (the same way APAC Events does its Nominate for Event): the rep presses Campaigns in the buttons at the top of the deal, flicks Yes or No for each campaign, and presses Done.

  • Try it: press Campaigns, flick Yes and No, press Done. Changing a Yes to a No takes the deal out of that campaign. Each change is saved at once, so there is no Save button. Use Phone to see the phone app version, and Deal for an Opportunity, a Risk, and a deal with no Primary HCP yet.
  • Which campaigns are shown: only those the deal can go into: active, covering the country of the deal's owner, covering the deal's portfolio, and with the deal's Primary HCP as a member. Nothing else is listed. ROSA deals are not in campaigns.
  • On a phone it opens full screen like Nominate for Event: Salesforce's own Cancel and the title at the top, the list in the middle, and Done fixed at the bottom. Cancel is Salesforce's and does not undo anything, because each Yes or No is already saved.
  • Phone slider (Robert, 2026-11-07): on a phone the Yes / No slider is 33% taller than on desktop (37 px instead of 28 px) so a finger tracks it easily; use Phone slider to compare. Each campaign shows its portfolio, then Campaign Manager and the name on a line of its own.
  • Behind the scenes there are two actions, one for desktop and one for phones, each shown only on its own device (as in APAC Events). The rep sees one Campaigns button on each.

Not shown: the campaign manager's side (Campaign Management is a separate screen). Changing the existing record page of Opportunities & Risks needs your own sign-off at build time. The phone is drawn at 390 pixels wide from your APAC Events screenshots and has not been tried in the Salesforce app.
