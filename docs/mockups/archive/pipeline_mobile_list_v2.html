PIPELINE Pipeline Opportunities & Risks
ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 2, invented data only. Robert chose B from version 1 (a folding section for each kind of deal) and asked for two changes: everything is folded when the screen opens, as on the desktop, and there is one more section to open: Charts. Three phones, left to right:

  • The opening screen: the Status slider, the search box, and four folded sections: Charts, Opportunities, Risks, ROSA (the counts show on the headings).
  • Charts open, as on the desktop: what each desktop summary panel shows: three figures (the deals, the revenue a year, the ones needing attention), the deals by stage as coloured chips, and by expected close quarter as bars.
  • Charts open, as real charts: the same figures, but by stage as horizontal bars and by close quarter as columns.
  • Try it: fold and open any section; press the Type slider inside Charts (a chart is for one kind of deal at a time, because the values are never added across Opportunities, Risks and ROSA); press the Status slider, and the charts and lists follow it. Scroll inside the phones. Theme for dark.

Not shown: a deal with another currency (it would be its own figure and its own chart, never added together), and tapping a bar or a chip to list those deals. The real charts would be drawn as small SVG pictures, like the graphs in My Case Bookings.
