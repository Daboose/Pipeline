ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

The My Case Bookings screen with live charts and a suggestion panel, in three columns that are the same for every portfolio: the chart (a third), the list of hospitals (a fifth) and the panel for the hospital you click (the rest). Hip has one hospital and knee has three, each in a different situation. The page below is the size of your window; the screen inside it scrolls. Invented data only (Dr Example Surgeon).

Try it:
  • Click a hospital in the list. The panel on the right shows what we found and what you can do: three choices at most, the same four places every time (the problem, the choices, the result, the button).
  • At Sample Day Surgery and Harbour View Hospital you can also add a competitor usage in the same save (pick the company, type the cases a year). At Example Private Hospital, the first choice adds the ZB usage and lets you keep, change or delete the competitor in one save. The ZB cases a year start at the suggestion from the bookings and can be changed.
  • Press Review change, then Confirm. Nothing is saved until Confirm. Next hospital moves on. Start again resets.
Worth knowing (the rule this shows): one usage per surgeon, hospital, portfolio and company, so ZB and a competitor each have their own usage at the same hospital. Not shown yet: CMFT and shoulder, hip stems and cups with different companies, the rest of the deal flyout behind it.
