ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 4, invented data only. The deal panel's four Yes / No sliders with green for Yes (Rep priority, State priority, MyMo) and amber for On hold, shown two ways side by side so you can compare them:

  • C1. Light fill (version 3): on Yes a solid highlight, a light fill behind it across the pill with a coloured edge, and coloured text. This is the Conversion bar's own look.
  • C2. Solid colour (from version 2): on Yes the whole pill fills solid green (solid amber for On hold) with a white highlight. The text is white on green and dark navy on amber, because white on amber is hard to read.
  • Try it: press No or Yes on any slider; the colour fades over 0.4 s as the highlight slides. Person shows a person who cannot edit (greyed). Theme for dark.

Not shown: the MyMo stage field, and Save and Cancel (unchanged).
