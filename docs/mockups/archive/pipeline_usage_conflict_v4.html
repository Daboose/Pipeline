ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

The My Case Bookings screen with live charts and a suggestion panel, in three columns that are the same for every portfolio: the chart (a third), the list of hospitals (a fifth) and the panel for the hospital you click (the rest). Hip has one hospital and knee has three, each in a different situation. The page below is the size of your window; the screen inside it scrolls. Invented data only (Dr Example Surgeon).

Try it:
  • Click a hospital in the list. The panel on the right shows what we found there and what you can do, always in the same four places: the problem, the choices, the result and the button.
  • Pick a choice, press Review change, then Confirm. Nothing is saved until Confirm. After a save, Next hospital moves on. Start again resets.
  • The three knee hospitals show the three situations: a competitor's usage, no usage, and a ZB usage that is out from the bookings.
  • Light / Dark switches the theme; resizing redraws the charts.
Worth knowing: a usage holds one company per line, so ZB and a competitor cannot both sit on the same line. Not shown yet: CMFT and shoulder, the rest of the deal flyout behind it.
