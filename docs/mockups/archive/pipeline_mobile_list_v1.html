PIPELINE Pipeline Opportunities & Risks
ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. The first screen of Pipeline on a phone: the list of my deals, as a sales rep like Lewis Dawson sees it (only their own). Drawn at 390 px wide, inside the Salesforce phone app's own top bar and bottom tab bar. Two ways to arrange the three kinds of deal, side by side:

  • A. One kind at a time: a “Type” slider (Opportunities, Risks, ROSA, each with its count) above the list, and one list underneath.
  • B. Everything in one scroll: a section for each kind, each with a heading and its count, that folds shut with a tap, like the lists on the desktop.
  • Both have: a “Status” slider (Open, Closed, Both; Open to start), a search box with the round clear button, and one card for each deal: the name (the surgeon, or the hospital for ROSA), the hospital, the stage and close quarter, the value a year, the stars (green rep priority, gold in a campaign) and the status pill when it says something (On Hold in an Open list, Closed Won or Closed Lost in a Closed list, all of them in Both).
  • Try it: press the sliders, type in the search box, fold a section (B), scroll inside the phone. Theme for dark. Tapping a deal opens it on the next screen (not drawn yet).

Not shown: the deal screen, and a way to sort (the list is by value, the biggest first, as on the desktop). The sliders are 37 px high, 33% taller than on the desktop, and the search text is 16 px so the iPhone does not zoom. The tab bar and top bar are Salesforce's own, drawn only to show the space they take.
