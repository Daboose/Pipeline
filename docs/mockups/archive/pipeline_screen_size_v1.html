ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Proposal: a Screen size setting. Here it offers five steps: 100% (as it is today), 90%, 80%, 70% and 60% (everything that much of its normal size: fonts, padding, pills, icons and tables, all together). The settings panel shows three ways to choose the step, side by side, for you to compare. It lives in the settings panel behind the cog and would be saved in each person's settings, so it follows them to any computer. Pipeline and Campaign Management would share the one choice. Under the hood it is one number (for example 0.8 for 80%) that scales the whole screen, so no component is changed one by one. The screens below use invented data and the same look as Pipeline today. The phone app (Pipeline Mobile) is not affected.

  • Try it: press the cog (top left of the app). Try Option A, the one-pill slider: drag the white handle, or press a step. Then try B and C. They all do the same thing, and the screen changes when you let go.
  • Try it: under "Pretend my screen is", pick "Zimmer laptop at 150%" (1280 px wide, as in the two screenshots). Step through 100%, 90%, 80% and so on and watch how much more fits.
  • Try it: press CAMPAIGN or PIPELINE at the top to see both screens at both sizes.
  • Try it: on the Pipeline screen, scroll down. The coloured heading bar and the column names still stay locked at the top at every step.
  • Not shown yet: the deal flyout, the other panels (Surgeon Usages, MyCase Bookings, Change selection), light mode. They would shrink the same way. The mockup has not been tried on a real Windows laptop; that is what UAT is for.
