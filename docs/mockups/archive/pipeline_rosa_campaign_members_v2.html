ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

A campaign whose members can be surgeons (they find implant opportunities and risks) and healthcare facilities (they find ROSA deals only), in one list, with one Add box (Robert chose this on 2026-11-25). Invented data.

  • Part 1: type ka in the Add box. The results come in two groups, Surgeons and Healthcare facilities, each with a round plus. Press a plus and the surgeon or facility joins the table in Part 2; a facility arrives with its ROSA deals, found only through the facility. A name already in the campaign has no plus (try ro).
  • Part 2 is the Maintain table. A facility's line has a small FACILITY chip and no rating plate; a surgeon's line has just the rating plate. A facility's ROSA deals sit under it. Press a round minus beside a name for the "Are you sure?" box, or a deal's round button to put it in or out.
  • Not shown yet: the Report list, the value chips and counts for ROSA, the Opportunities / Risks / Include Closed chips, "Widen search", the country rule for a facility, light mode.
