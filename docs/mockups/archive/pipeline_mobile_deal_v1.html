PIPELINE Pipeline Opportunities & Risks
ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. The deal screen on the phone, read-only: what opens when a rep taps a card on the list. Drawn at 390 px wide inside the Salesforce phone app's own bars. Top to bottom:

  • A “My deals” back button of its own (Salesforce's own back arrow leaves the Pipeline tab altogether, so ours returns to the list).
  • The name (the surgeon; the hospital for a ROSA deal), with the amber On Hold pill when it is on hold, and the kind of deal under it.
  • The summary line as a grid of tiles: Owner, Portfolio, Value a year, Close quarter, Last posted and Last stage move (coloured by how old), Deal age and Status.
  • The stage, which does not fit across a phone as on the desktop (seven stages). Three ways, one on each phone: A. the same one-pill slider, scrolling sideways with the current stage in view; B. the stage's name large over a bar of steps; C. one coloured row that names the stage and opens the list of stages (press it to see the list).
  • The deal's details (Primary HCP, hospital, expected closing quarter and year, annual value, units, brand, and the Yes / No fields as plain words), then three rows to open Surgeon Usages, MyCase Bookings and Campaigns, then Chatter below (a placeholder).
  • Try it: Deal switches between an open opportunity, an opportunity on hold, one won, one lost, a risk and a ROSA deal; the stage colours and the pills follow. Theme for dark. Scroll inside the phones.

Not shown: editing (the next step: the stage tapped to change, the Yes / No sliders, the fields), Chatter, and the three screens behind the rows. Nothing here saves anything.
