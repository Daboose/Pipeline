ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. Two changes to the phone's deal screen, shown before anything is built.

  • 1. The Yes / No sliders without the fill behind the title (phone 4). Today, with Yes on, the whole pill turns solid green, label end included, which looks too much like the full “Back to My Pipeline” bar. Two ways to keep the label end plain: Green thumb (the label and the track stay as they are and the thumb turns green for Yes, amber for On hold; No is the blue thumb, so the pair reads blue / green) or Green track (the track fills with the colour and the thumb is white, but the label end stays plain). The switch above the phones shows these and today's look. Tap any slider to flip it. The same would apply to the In campaign sliders.
  • 2. The surgeon's usage rating in the header (phones 1 to 3): a chip in the metal of the rating plates, with just the word (Platinum, Gold, Silver or Bronze; grey “No rating”) and no portfolio name, for the deal's own area. A puts it at the right of the name, where the arrow pointed. B makes it a tile in the grid and takes out the Portfolio tile, because “Opportunity · Knee” under the name already says the portfolio. Use the Rating switch to see each colour.

Not shown: how it would be built. The rating is already read for the Surgeon Usages screen (the Apex that gives the usages gives the four ratings), so there is nothing new on the server; a ROSA deal has no surgeon, so it would show no chip. The desktop's sliders stay as they are.
