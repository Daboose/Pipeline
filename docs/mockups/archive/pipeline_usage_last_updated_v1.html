ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

What it shows: the Surgeon Usages screen (and the Usage Card on an Account page) with two changes. 1. The line under the plates that said when the usages were last changed ("Usages last changed 1 Jan 2025 (23 months ago). Worth checking they are still right.") is gone, because with many usages it does not say which one. 2. A thin new column, Last updated, sits after Procedures and technologies, next to Edit and Delete on the right, and gives the date that usage was last updated, as day, month and year in two digits each (DD/MM/YY). A date within the last year is in the normal text colour, the same as the company, product and cases; a date more than a year old is in amber. A hip usage (the stem and the cup are one usage) shows its date once, on its first line, beside the stem. The mock date today is 20/11/26. Invented data.

Try it:
  • Look down the Last updated column, now the last one before Edit and Delete: the amber dates are the usages not changed for over a year.
  • Press Light or Dark; press Full screen (top right).
Not shown yet: editing or adding a usage (the date is for viewing, so nothing is drawn in that column then), the "changed just now" note that appears after a change, and the Account page.
