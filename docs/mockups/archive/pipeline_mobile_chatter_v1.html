ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. Chatter on the phone, on its own screen. You suggested Chatter be a screen of its own that slides in, like Surgeon Usages, MyCase Bookings and Campaigns will be, because reading a conversation and writing in it needs room. The first phone shows how a rep gets there; the next three show the same Chatter screen with the writing done three ways; the last shows the writing page with the keyboard up.

  • Phone 1, the way in: the deal screen has a row for each of its screens: Surgeon Usages, MyCase Bookings and Campaigns (all still “Soon”) and Chatter, with how many posts and how long since the last. Tapping it slides the Chatter screen in over the deal (0.4 s, like the search page), with its own “‹ Back to deal” bar.
  • What the screen does (all of what the desktop does): shows the newest posts first, each with its replies; a post can be replied to; a new post can be written; @ and a name mentions someone, picked from a list (active staff only). Posts and replies are made as the person, in the deal's own feed.
  • Phone 2, A: write at the top of the feed. A “Write a post…” box opens in place; Reply opens a box under the post. Like Salesforce's own feed.
  • Phone 3, B: a box locked at the bottom. Like a messaging app: a one-line box and a round send button stay at the bottom while the feed scrolls. Reply puts “Replying to…” above it. It has to share the bottom with Salesforce's floating bars and the keyboard, which is the hardest place on the phone (see the notes on keyboards).
  • Phone 4, C: a writing page that slides in (my suggestion). A full-width “+ New post” button at the top and a Reply under each post slide in the same page we use for the lookups: a big box, the original post above it when replying, and a locked Cancel / Post bar. Typing @ shows the matching people above the keyboard. One extra tap, but the writing never fights the feed for the screen, and it is the pattern the edit screen already uses.
  • Phone 5: C's writing page with the keyboard up and a mention being typed, so the room can be judged.
  • Try it: on phones 2, 3 and 4 press Write a post, New post or Reply, type something (on 4 and 5 try “@pri”) and press Post: it joins the top of the feed. Theme for dark.

Not shown yet: an error when a post is refused (it would show above the box), likes, attachments and the older posts beyond the newest (the desktop shows the newest too). A manager's pre-filled request to check a surgeon's usages stays on the desktop. The deal screen's Chatter placeholder card goes away.
