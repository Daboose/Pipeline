PIPELINE MOCKUP: DARKER PANEL BACKGROUNDS (CHOOSE YOUR DATA)

ABOUT THIS MOCKUP
Eight choices for how dark the Choose Your Data panel could be in dark mode. Each card is the same panel (invented content) on the dimmed, blurred page, with a different background. Set 1 is today's colour. Everything else about the panel stays the same, including the blue line along the bottom.
TRY IT
  • Look at how each one holds the amber titles, the indigo chips and the boxes (Where, What, Stage). The colour codes under each name are the panel, the boxes and the input fields.
  • Click a card to see it on its own, full width. Click again to go back to all eight.
NOT SHOWN YET
Light mode (a separate choice), and the rest of the screen. Whichever you pick would also apply to the settings panel unless you say otherwise.
