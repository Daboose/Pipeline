ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

The import in Sales Campaign Management when a campaign can hold surgeons and healthcare facilities in one list. Each pasted line is matched against both kinds together; nothing is chosen for the person. Invented data.

  • Part 1 is the paste screen: only its words change (surgeons and hospitals).
  • Part 2 is the check screen. A facility shows the small FACILITY chip beside its name and no hospitals (a facility has none). Try it: tick or untick a row; press Confirm on a Possible match; on the Elizabeth Mount row, which matches a surgeon AND two facilities, open the list and pick one, then Confirm; on the Siti Abdullah row press Look up and type ka or mount: the results come in two groups, Surgeons and Healthcare facilities. Press Skip, then Undo. The counts and the Add button follow.
  • "Create this surgeon" stays in the look-up as today (surgeons only); there is no way to create a facility here. Not shown: Widen search working, sorting and resizing the columns, the leave-without-adding warning.

Part 1 Paste the list

Sales Campaigns / Knee and ROSA Launch / Import members

Paste a list of surgeons and hospitals, one per line, straight from Excel

Add the hospital after a surgeon's name (a comma, a dash or a tab) to help with common names. Titles such as Dr are ignored, and so is the order of the words. The English name is searched too. Each line is checked against surgeons and healthcare facilities. Nothing is saved on this step: the next screen shows what each line matched, and you confirm every row.

Part 2 Check the names

Exact matches are ready to go. For every other row, choose the person or facility and press Confirm. Only rows with a tick in the Add column are added.
