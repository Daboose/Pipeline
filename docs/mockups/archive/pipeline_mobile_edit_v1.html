PIPELINE Pipeline Opportunities & Risks
ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. Editing a deal on the phone. The deal screen you have been using is read-only; this shows how a rep changes a deal. Three phones show three moments of the same screen, and all three work.

  • No Edit button. The fields are there to change, as on the desktop. Nothing is saved until Save is pressed.
  • The stage list can now be picked from. Tap a stage and the row takes its colour and name (a draft); the list folds shut.
  • The first change folds a locked Save / Cancel bar up from the bottom edge of the page; it stays there while the page scrolls. Every field you have changed has its label and outline in indigo. Cancel puts everything back; Save keeps it and the bar folds away with “Saved.”.
  • The fields are the desktop's for an opportunity: people and hospitals are looked up (tapping opens a search page that slides in, with its own “‹ Back to deal” bar), quarter and year, value and currency, units, brand pills, the four Yes / No sliders (On hold amber, the rest green), the MyMo stage that folds open only while MyMo is Yes, and the background information. Text is 16 px so the iPhone does not zoom; every control is 44 px high or more.
  • Leaving with unsaved changes (press ‹ Back to My Pipeline on phone 2) asks first: Stay or Leave.

Try it: phone 1 has nothing changed: change anything and watch the Save bar come up. Phone 2 is part way through a change: press Save, Cancel or Back. Phone 3 has the search for the Primary HCP open: type “mei” and tap a name.

Not shown yet: risks and ROSA (they use their own field lists, as on the desktop), Chatter, Surgeon Usages, MyCase Bookings and Campaigns, the error messages Salesforce sends back (they would appear above the fields, as on the desktop), and fields a person is not allowed to edit (shown as plain text). The list of choices (quarter, brand and so on) will be read from Salesforce, not typed in.
