PIPELINE Pipeline Opportunities & Risks Accounts
ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. It shows how a sales rep puts one deal into one or many campaigns, which Robert asked for on 2026-11-06 so that nobody has to work with a link record. The deal is shown in the Pipeline deal flyout (as it is today) with a new Campaigns button, and on a phone.

  • Try it: press Campaigns in the top row of the deal. The panel slides in from the right. Flick a switch to put the deal in that campaign, flick it back to take it out. It saves straight away. Watch the number on the button and the “In campaigns” box under Details.
  • Only the right campaigns are offered: active, covering the country of the deal's owner, covering the deal's portfolio, and with the deal's Primary HCP (the surgeon) as a member. Press “campaigns are not offered” to see each one left out and the reason, so a rep knows what to ask the campaign manager for.
  • Switch the deal below: an Opportunity, a Risk and a ROSA deal all work the same way (all three have a Primary HCP); the fourth deal has no Primary HCP yet, so there is nothing to match.
  • Phone: choose Phone to see the same panel as a full-screen sheet rising from the bottom.

Not shown yet: the deal's other panels, the campaign manager's side (Campaign Management is a separate mockup), and what the person sees with no access. The phone has not been tried in the Salesforce mobile app: a component button there needs its own wrapper and has to be tested on a phone before it can be called working.
