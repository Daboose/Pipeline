ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. Robert's request (2026-11-09): the little chevrons in the Team picker of Change selection are hard to hit. Make the picker work like the ROSTRA team selector: a big click area for the chevron that lights up as you point at it.

  • Left, Today: the picker as it is now. The chevron is an 18 px caret, with nothing to show where to click.
  • Right, Proposed: the ROSTRA way. The chevron sits in a 44 px wide block, the full height of the row and flush with its left edge. The block lights up when the pointer is over it, and the chevron turns down when the team is open. Rows are 44 px tall.
  • Try it: (1) click chevrons on both sides, miss a little to the left and right and compare; (2) click a team's name to pick it (it turns indigo); (3) type in the search box to filter; (4) switch Light and Dark above. The line under each panel counts your chevron clicks and row picks.
  • Not shown yet: the rest of Change selection (owner, country, "Include teams below"). Only the tree changes; picking, searching and "My team" work as they do today.
