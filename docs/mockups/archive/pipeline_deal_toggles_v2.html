ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 2, invented data only. The four Yes / No sliders in the deal panel (On hold, Rep priority, State priority, MyMo) with the label inside the pill, as built in 0.86. Robert asked that when one is set to Yes it goes a full colour, the same green as the Conversion bar, for the whole pill, with the text going green too. A No stays as it is. Three versions side by side:

  • Today (0.86): the highlight is the usual indigo for both No and Yes.
  • C1. Like the Conversion bar: on Yes the highlight is solid green, a light green fill sits behind it across the whole pill with a green edge, and the label and “No” turn green. This is the Conversion bar's own treatment (the green is the same #16a34a).
  • C2. Solid green: on Yes the whole pill fills solid green with white text, and the highlight is white with a green YES. Bolder.
  • Try it: press No or Yes on any slider; the colour fades over 0.4 s as the highlight slides. Person shows a person who cannot edit (greyed). Use Theme for dark.

Not shown: the MyMo stage field, and the Save and Cancel (unchanged). Colour meaning: green is “on”, so it also fits On hold, even though being on hold is not good news; say so if On hold should be another colour.
