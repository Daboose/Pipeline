PIPELINE Pipeline Sales Campaigns
ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

What it shows: the Rating column of a Pipeline list, with the Platinum, Gold, Silver and Bronze chips exactly as they are, and the Not rated chip drawn five ways, one list each: Today (the near-black fill), then four quieter ones. 1 a soft grey fill with grey words. 2 an outline only, no fill. 3 a pale slate with the same soft edge as the metal chips. 4 a faint fill with a dashed outline. Every Not rated chip keeps the same size and the words NOT RATED; only its strength changes, so the metal chips are what the eye goes to first. Invented data only.

Try it:
  • Look down the Rating column of each list and notice which chips you see first.
  • Press Light or Dark (the quieter chips change with the theme; the metal chips do not).
  • Press Full screen (top right).
Not shown yet: the Campaign list and the value chips along the top of the campaign screen, which use the same Not rated chip.
