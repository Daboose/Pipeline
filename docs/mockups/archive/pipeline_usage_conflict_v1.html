ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Shows the knee card in My Case Bookings when the surgeon already has a knee usage at a hospital, but for a competitor. Today that card only says "Usages list no ZB knee" and offers "Open Surgeon Usages". Here the card first says what the problem is, then offers the fix hospital by hospital. All names and numbers are invented.

Try it:
  • In each hospital block, click the choices. The "Now" and "After" boxes show what the usage line would become.
  • Choose Change the competitor's cases and type a number.
  • Press Review changes, then Confirm. Nothing is written until Confirm. Start again resets.
  • The Light / Dark button switches the theme.
Worth knowing: a knee usage holds one company and one case number per surgeon and hospital, so ZB and a competitor cannot both sit on the same hospital's usage. That is why "Replace" is the main choice and "Keep the competitor" records no ZB cases.
