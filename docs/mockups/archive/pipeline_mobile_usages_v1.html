PIPELINE Pipeline Opportunities & Risks
ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. Surgeon Usages on the phone, as a screen of its own (the last “Soon” row). It does what the desktop panel does: shows which companies' products the deal's surgeon uses, hospital by hospital, and lets a rep who is allowed to change them edit, delete and add usages.

  • Phone 1, the way in: a Surgeon Usages row next to Chatter, Campaigns and MyCase Bookings, saying how long since the usages were last updated (amber when they are old, two years or more). Tapping it slides the screen in, with its own “‹ Back to deal” bar.
  • Phone 2, the screen: the four ratings across the top as the metal plates from the Account page card (Hip, Knee, Shoulder, CMFT; Platinum, Gold, Silver or Bronze, with 4, 3, 2 or 1 stars; the deal's own area outlined in indigo; grey for no rating). Two sizes to choose from: 4 across (small) or 2 × 2, wide; in both the word leads and the star symbol is small; a freshness line (“Usages updated 3 months ago”, with the warning and “Update these usages the next time you see this surgeon” when they are old); a + Add usage button; then one card for each usage under its hospital: Hip shows stems and cups, Shoulder anatomical and reverse, Knee and CMFT one line; each line the company (ZB, gold, is Zimmer Biomet) and the cases a year. The deal's own area has a bar down its left edge.
  • Editing is one usage at a time (phone 3): tap a usage card and a page slides in with the hospital and portfolio, each line's company (a dropdown) and cases a year (the number keyboard), a red Delete this usage, and a locked Cancel / Save bar. Delete asks first (“This cannot be undone”). Nothing is saved until Save.
  • Adding (phone 4): + Add usage slides in a page: the hospital (a search page, like the lookups), the portfolio (a dropdown of the ones you may add), the lines for that portfolio, and Cancel / Add. A surgeon never gets two usages for the same hospital and portfolio: adding one that exists opens the existing one for editing and says so. The record type is chosen by Salesforce from your country and the portfolio, as on the desktop.
  • Phone 5: two other situations, shown one under the other only to save room. A rep sees just one: a ROSA deal with no Primary HCP (A; an opportunity or a risk always has one, so only ROSA can), or a surgeon with no usages yet (B). If Salesforce refuses a change (usages are shared by rules, and a few are shared read-only, so a person can occasionally see a usage they may not change), its message shows above the Cancel / Save bar and nothing is lost, as on the deal form.
  • Try it: tap a usage on phone 2, change a company or a number, press Save, or Delete. Tap + Add usage, tap the hospital, press Add. Use the Usages switch below to see the old warning. Theme for dark.

Not shown: the desktop's “Ask Lewis to check them” (a manager's pre-filled Chatter request, desktop only, as with Chatter). Reads and saves with the same Apex as the desktop (getUsages, saveUsage, deleteUsage, addUsage), no new server code; the company lists are read from Salesforce for each record type. The rating plates are the metal plates from the Account page's Usage Card, drawn in CSS only (no images), adapted for the phone in two sizes: switch between them above.
