PIPELINE MOCKUP v11: MANAGER SCREEN

ABOUT THIS MOCKUP
The manager web screen: choose which deals to look at (the data selectors), then see the totals, the stages, the quarters, each rep, and the deals, with the 30 and 90 day warning colours. All names, teams, deals and numbers are invented (the brand and portfolio names are the real ones). Nothing is built in Salesforce.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V23
  • The settings panel has its own little pipe in the top corner, like "Choose Your Data": "YOUR" in amber and "SETTINGS" in white on the pipe. Click the cog (top left) to see it. The options bar has Settings heading to compare "YOUR SETTINGS", just "SETTINGS" on the pipe, or the old plain text.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V22
  • Shorter names on the value total so the three totals fit on one row in every panel, even with all three types on: Revenue · AUD, Risk value · AUD and ROSA value · AUD.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V21
  • One panel for every type, always the same size. A single type sits in the same panel as in the two-type view (half the width, the blank space on the right); two types share the width; three types are three equal panels (ROSA has blank space at the bottom, as asked). The Team table always has its own line below.
  • The three totals now fit on one row in every panel (Opportunities was wrapping "Needs attention" onto a second row).
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V20
  • A new layout, "Dash", is the default. With two or three types on, each type is a column side by side, so all three fit on one screen above the lists. With one type on, the totals run across the top and the stages, close quarters and the Team table sit side by side, so there are no dead boxes. Stage chips now have the name on the left and the value on the right.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V19
  • The totals are readable and fit their text. Open deals, expected annual revenue and needs attention now have big numbers, bigger labels, and no empty space inside the box; "needs attention" lists its three counts on three lines.
  • By expected close quarter is a fraction of its old size, with bigger text, short bars and only as wide as its text, instead of stretching across the screen. Nothing is widened to fill the screen; the space to the right stays empty for now.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V18
  • Medium text is the one size (the large option is gone), and no wasted width: every stage chip is as wide as the longest one (the longest stage name plus its value), and the box around the chips hugs them instead of spreading across the screen.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V17
  • No more funnel. Each type's stages are now five (or four) plain coloured chips stacked on top of each other, all the same width, slimmer than before. Each chip is one line: the stage name with the count in brackets, then the value, like Plan (3) A$649k. The colours stay. The options bar has Chip text: large or medium.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V16
  • The text is the point, so it is much bigger. The sides come in 28 (your pick) by default. Inside each stage: the value, big, and the stage name with the number of deals in brackets, like Plan (3), with far less padding. Four text sizes under Funnel text: Medium, Large (default), Extra large, and one line with the name and value side by side.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V15
  • The middle of the funnel is narrower ("a fat bucket" before). Plan still comes in from full width, the stages after it are the same narrower rectangle, and the last stage tapers in a little. The options bar has Funnel sides: narrower (the new default), narrowest, or the v15 width to compare.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V14
  • The funnel is drawn exactly as described: Plan comes in gently from full width; Action, Commit, 1st Case and the rest are plain rectangles the same width as the bottom of Plan; the last stage tapers in a little at the bottom. The shape no longer changes with the values; the colours, the value, the stage name and the deal count stay.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V13
  • A wider funnel with readable stage names. The shape you liked (wide at the top, coming in slightly at the bottom) now never gets narrower than a floor, so every stage name fits. Two widths under Stage view: "wide" and "wider".
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V12
  • The stages are a real funnel now, a different colour for each stage, with the value big inside each stage and the stage name and number of deals under it. Three funnel designs are in the options bar under Stage view (Salesforce style, even steps, width follows value). The same three are side by side on pipeline_choose_stage_view.html.
  • The on/off buttons are the round-tick look you chose; the other four were removed.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V11
  • A separate list for each type. Opportunities, Risks and ROSA each get their own list at the bottom with the columns that suit them (ROSA: facility, deal type, sales type, ROSA Growth $, close date, install date). Use Deals list in the options bar to flip back to the one shared list and compare.
  • ROSA looks like the real thing: the deal types are Capital Sale, Rental, Placement, ROSA-E and ROSA-T, and it only has rep priority and state priority (no national priority).
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V10 (SIZES)
  • The panels were too big and left a lot of empty space. Four new ways to lay out each type's totals, stages and close quarters, in the options bar under Screen layout (the first one, "As v10", is the old size so you can compare): Band (one wide row per type, stages and quarters side by side), Columns (Opportunities, Risks and ROSA side by side, everything for a type in one narrow column) and Strip (the smallest: one slim card per type).
  • Try it: Change selection, switch all three types on, Apply, then flip Screen layout and see how much fits above the fold on your laptop. Stages in Band and Columns are slimmer and show on one line each.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE V9
  • ROSA is in. There are now three on/off buttons: Opportunities, Risks and ROSA. ROSA uses its own stages (Sales Pipeline, Surgeon Engagement, Executive Presentation, Negotiation, Closed Won, Deal Lost) and its own value (ROSA Growth $). Portfolio and brand do not apply to ROSA.
  • Nothing is mashed together any more. Each type you switch on gets its own section on the main screen, with its own totals, its own stage component and its own close quarter component. The stage component is much narrower.
  • Three ways to show the stages (mockup option "Stage view"): compact bars, a funnel whose width follows the number of deals, and a funnel that steps down evenly. Count and value sit inside each stage.
TRY IT
  • Change selection, switch Risks and ROSA on, and Apply. Then flip Stage view in the options bar.
  • Look at the Team table (a value column per type) and the Deals table (a Type column).
NOT SHOWN YET
Editing a deal, the Chatter feed, the mobile rep screen, the surgeon summary, usages and med ed events. Brand lists come from the real record types for Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Malaysia and Thailand. In this mockup all lists (brands, stages, countries, teams, quarters) are typed in; in the real tool they are read live from Salesforce, so a new brand or stage appears on its own. Colours (mint = current, orange = needs update, red = very old) are proposals.
