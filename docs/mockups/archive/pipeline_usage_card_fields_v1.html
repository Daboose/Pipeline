ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

What it shows. The Usage Card on a surgeon's Account page, redrawn so that each usage shows and edits the same fields as PROD's own usage layouts for Hip, Knee, Shoulders and CMFT. Procedure types and supporting technologies are chips along the bottom (procedure types purple, supporting technologies green, a gap between them), so no heading is needed.

Try it:

  1. Press the pencil on the North Hospital Hip. Every field opens for editing, a red * marks the mandatory ones, and the chips show all the choices (a filled chip is chosen; press one to switch it). Clear the Stem company: Save greys out. Change the company: the product list changes with it.
  2. Press the pencil on Shoulders (Anatomical and Reverse, all optional) and on CMFT (Segment, Company, Product, Cases mandatory).
  3. Press Add usage, choose a hospital, then Hip: the Cup block is greyed until the Stem company and product are filled (no usage can be cups only). Choose Knee, Shoulders and CMFT to see their fields. Fill the mandatory fields and press Save: the new usage appears under its hospital.

Not shown yet. Delete, the "Please wait" cover, the hospital view, the surgeon's rating plates and bookings (unchanged), and other countries' layouts (this is the ANZ one). The hospital box here is a plain list; the real one searches. Company names and the chip words are read from PROD; the surgeon, hospitals and numbers are invented, and the products are invented.
