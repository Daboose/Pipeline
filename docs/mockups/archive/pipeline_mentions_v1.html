PIPELINE MOCKUP: CHATTER @MENTIONS (v1)

ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Robert asked for mentions that behave like Chatter's own: a suggestion list as you type, and a picked person becomes a chip. This is the Chatter box from the deal flyout, on its own. Everything here is invented (people, deal, posts); nothing is sent or saved.

TRY IT
  • Click in the box and type @re: nothing yet except a hint, because the list starts after three letters. Add one more letter (@ree, @reeves) and the people who match appear.
  • Matching is by any part of the name, either order, with the matching letters in bold: @reeves and @rob ree both find Robert Reeves; @shaun finds Shaun Whittal. A small slip in spelling still finds the person, but only when nobody matches exactly, and the first letter must be right (try @whital for Shaun Whittal, or @vercow for Ray Vercoe).
  • Move down the list with the arrow keys and press Enter (or Tab), or click a name. Esc closes the list.
  • The name becomes a chip: bold, coloured, with a small X. Click the X to take it off.
  • Type some more after the chip, then delete backwards with Backspace: the text goes, then the space, and the next Backspace takes the whole chip in one go.
  • The line under the box says exactly who will be notified. Press Post and the post appears with the mention in colour. Reply on a post gives a reply box that works the same way.
  • The dark / light control and the full screen icon behave as in the other mockups.
CHOICES I MADE FOR YOU TO CHECK (not yet confirmed)
  • "Clever" matching means: any part of the name, either order, and (only if nothing matches exactly) one small slip in spelling after the first letter, so @warr gives Warren Ballinger and never Darren Agius. It does not turn sounds or spelled-out letters into names (so @rdoublee would find nobody). If you want that kind of guessing, say so.
  • Only active people are offered. Each shows their title or team beside the name so two similar names can be told apart.
  • The chip carries the person's Id, so the right person is mentioned. Typing a name that is not picked from the list stays plain text (no mention, no notification).
  • A space after a chip is added for you so you can carry on typing.
