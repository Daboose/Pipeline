ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1: one component for any Account page. It is a Lightning web component placed on the Lightning record page of an Account (not a tab, not a full-screen app), in the two-thirds column. It behaves differently depending on the account it sits on: on a person account (a surgeon) it manages their usages; on a business account (a hospital) it only shows case totals by surgeon. Light theme so it blends into the page. Invented data only.

Try it:
  • Use the yellow Mockup controls to open the record page of Dr Whitfield (a physician), Dr Okafor (an emerging consultant), Dr Nair (New Zealand, no MyCase bookings) and Royal Northside Hospital (a business account).
  • Surgeon: Edit a line (company and cases), Delete a line (asks inline), Add usage (folds open; pick a hospital, try adding a line that already exists to see the one-usage rule).
  • "Viewer can change usages" in the controls switches the Add, Edit and Delete buttons off, as for someone without permission.
  • Rating graphic switches the four rating tiles between today's dots, the metal plates (Robert's choice, 2026-11-05; from usage_rating_cards_v1.html) and the five graphic styles from the style page (usage_rating_graphics_v1.html). It opens on the metal plates. Dr Whitfield has Gold, Silver and Bronze; Dr Okafor and Dr Nair show other mixes.
  • "Viewer can see MyCase": No shows someone with no access to the MyCase objects (for example a New Zealand user): the bookings table is not on the surgeon's page at all, and the hospital page has no MyCase switch, only the usage records. No error, no gap, no mention.
  • Hospital: switch between MyCase bookings and Usage records (the two ways the totals could be counted; to choose between). The usage records view splits every surgery type into ZB (Zimmer Biomet) and Other companies; MyCase holds ZB surgeries only, so it has no split. Click a surgeon's name to open their page.

Not shown yet: the phone, dark mode, the real company choices (they come from each usage's record type), the real hospital search, the grey boxes standing in for the rest of the page. Assumed, to confirm: ratings are only recalculated by Salesforce in the background, so after a change the component says so instead of changing the rating at once.
