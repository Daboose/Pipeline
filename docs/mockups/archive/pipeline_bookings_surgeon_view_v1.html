ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

What it shows: the MyCase Bookings screen of a ROSA deal (a hospital), with a slider beside the heading to change the graph: Hospital Total (today's graph) and Surgeon Total (the same cases split by the surgeon who did them, month by month). The Hip, Knee, Shoulder and CMFT chips now switch on and off and change whichever graph is showing; the big number on the right counts only the types switched on. Three ways to draw Surgeon Total are shown, to choose one: A a row of bars per surgeon, B one bar per month coloured by surgeon, C a grid of numbers. Invented data.

Try it:
  • Slide between Hospital Total and Surgeon Total.
  • Press Shoulder and CMFT to switch them off: the bars, numbers and the total change. Press again to switch them back on.
  • On Surgeon Total, use Surgeon Total option above to compare A, B and C. Hover over a bar or a number to see its detail.
Not shown yet: a hospital with many surgeons (how many to show before the rest go under "Others"), whether the slider and the chips are remembered next time, light mode is drawn but not checked.
