ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

What it shows: the large rating plates of the Usage Card (Hip, Knee, Shoulder, CMFT), the same width and metal as today, shown with every rating (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze and No rating) in two rows of four, drawn five ways one under another so the category can be compared: Today (the category in small letters), then four ideas that make the category big and easy to read and bring the rating and its stars closer together, so there is room for it. A a larger category on top, with the star coin, the rating and the stars gathered under it. B the category on its own engraved strip across the top. C a very large category, with the stars beside the rating. D the category, rating and stars in a block on the left, with a big star coin centred on the right. The plates keep their height. Invented ratings.

Try it:
  • Compare the category on each plate at reading size, then press Light or Dark (the plates look the same in both).
  • Move the mouse over a plate: the sheen still sweeps across.
  • Press Full screen (top right) for the whole screen.
