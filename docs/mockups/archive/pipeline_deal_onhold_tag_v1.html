ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. When a deal is On hold, the words “On hold” appear in amber at the top of the deal panel, next to the surgeon's name, the same amber as the On hold slider. Three ways to show it, side by side, on the deal panel's header as it is today:

  • A. Solid amber pill: amber fill, dark text. Matches the solid amber On hold slider in the Details.
  • B. Light amber pill: a light amber fill with an amber edge and amber text. Quieter; the look of the Conversion bar.
  • C. Amber words: just the words “On hold” in amber, no pill. The quietest.
  • Try it: press On hold: Yes and the marker fades in beside the name (0.4 s); press No and it fades out, and the name moves back. In the real panel this would follow the On hold slider in the Details, even before the change is saved.

Not shown: the rest of the panel. The marker only appears for deals that have an On hold field (opportunities); risks and ROSA deals have none.
