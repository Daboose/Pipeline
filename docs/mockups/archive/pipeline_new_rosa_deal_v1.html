ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

What it shows: the screen for a new ROSA deal, opened from the campaign list when a healthcare facility shows "No deal yet". It is the same flyout as the surgeon's New Deal, named for the facility. A ROSA deal has no portfolio to choose: the only choice that starts the form is the country (it decides the record type), taken at once when the person works in one country. Then the form looks like an existing ROSA deal, as Robert's screenshot: the stage slider (starting at Sales Pipeline), the Healthcare Facility (filled in, changeable), an optional Primary HCP, ROSA deal type, ROSA sales type, ROSA Growth $ and currency, owner, expected closing quarter and year, install and close dates, the three platform lists, the two priority switches and the background information. Mandatory fields have a red *: the Healthcare Facility, ROSA Growth $, the owner, and the closing quarter and year (not in the past). Invented data; the lists are typed in here (the real ones are read live from Salesforce).

Try it:
  • Under Several countries press Yes: the form waits for a Country. Pick one and the form appears. With No the country is taken at once.
  • Press the stage names to move the stage; press Hip, Knee or Shoulder (more than one can be on).
  • Press the round x on the Healthcare Facility, Primary HCP or Owner to change it.
  • Leave ROSA Growth $ empty (Save stays grey), fill it in and press Save deal.
Decided: a new deal made here is added to the campaign being looked at (as the surgeon's is). Not shown yet: what the flyout does after Save, the slide-in motion, the real lists.
