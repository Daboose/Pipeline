ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

The deal flyout, with the usages and surgeries taken off the deal page and given a panel each. The deal page keeps its header, stage path, key figures and bottom bar. Two new buttons in the middle of the bottom bar, Surgeon Usages and My Case Bookings, open a panel over the deal. The header and the bottom bar stay as they are, so Save, Cancel, Next and Close are always there. Two ways of laying the panels over the deal are shown, as Style 1 and Style 2. The deal flyout here is wider than today (96% of the screen instead of 88%), and a panel covers everything under the header, including the key-figures row. All names and numbers are invented.

Try it:
  • Click Surgeon Usages or My Case Bookings at the bottom to open the panel. Click the same button again, the cross, or the dimmed deal behind (Style 1) to close it.
  • With one panel open, click the other button: the panel swaps.
  • Switch between Style 1 (slides in from the right, the deal peeks out on the left) and Style 2 (rises from the bottom bar as a card, the deal steps back behind it).
  • In Usages: Edit usages turns the numbers and companies into boxes, and Add usage opens a form. In it, search for a hospital that is not in the list (try "north" or "bay"), pick a portfolio, and Add: the new hospital appears in the table.
  • In My Case Bookings: switch between By type, By month and List, and click a type in the list view to filter it.
  • Dark and Light at the top; both are built.
Not shown yet: saving an edited usage (the buttons only change the picture), booking a new surgery, replying in Chatter, and the phone layout.
