About this mockup

What it shows. Whether the brushed-metal artwork (gold, platinum, bronze, silver sheet, Adobe Stock) can be used to make glamorous rating cards for the surgeon's account page. Yes, it can. The four metal colours here were sampled straight from the sheet and rebuilt in CSS, so nothing heavy is loaded (the SVG itself is 46 MB and could never go into Salesforce). Two card styles are shown; the drawings are placeholders for the real rating tile.

  • Style switches between the metal plate, the dark card with a metal rim, and both.
  • Surgeon changes which ratings the account strip shows (including a surgeon with some portfolios unrated).
  • Hover a card (on a computer) and a sheen sweeps across it.
  • Robert chose the metal plate (2026-11-05); the dark card is kept only for comparison.
  • The strip is sized as it would sit in the two-thirds column of the account page.

Not shown yet. The rest of the usage component (see usage_account_component_v1.html), the phone, and any wording; all names and ratings are invented. Which metal is which rank is as Robert said: Platinum highest, then Gold, Silver, Bronze. Rank is also told by the stars (4, 3, 2, 1), so it never depends on colour alone.
