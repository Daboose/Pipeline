ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

What it shows: the deal screen of a ROSA deal at a hospital, with the MyCase Bookings button now offered and its panel open: the surgeries at that hospital over the last 12 whole months, one stacked bar per month, split into Hip, Knee, Shoulder and CMFT. The Surgeon Usages button stays hidden for ROSA deals. All names and numbers are invented.

Try it:
  • Move the mouse over any coloured part of a bar: it names the month, the type and the number of cases, with the month's total.
  • Press Light or Dark to see both themes.
  • Press Full screen (top right) for the whole screen; press it again to leave.
  • Resize the window: the chart refits the space.
Not shown yet: this month so far, primary and revision, a hospital with no surgeries, the loading state, and what a deal with no hospital shows.
