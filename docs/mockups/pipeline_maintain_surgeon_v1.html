ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Two proposals for the Maintain mode of the campaign view in Pipeline: taking a surgeon out of the campaign (a round minus beside the surgeon's name, then an "Are you sure?" box) and putting a surgeon in (the Search box becomes an Add box in Maintain). Invented data. Row heights are measured live, not drawn by eye.

  • Part 1 shows three places for the surgeon's minus, side by side, so you can choose. In every one, the surgeon's line and each deal's line are the same height (54 px; the measurement is printed on each card, and the surgeon line keeps its 3 px pink line above it).
  • Try it: press a surgeon's minus. A box asks "Are you sure you want to remove this surgeon from the campaign?" with Proceed and Cancel. Cancel changes nothing; Proceed takes the surgeon, and their deals' lines, off the list (it is saved at once, as other presses are).
  • Part 2 shows the Add box. Try it: in the Maintain toolbar type two letters (for example ka) in the box labelled Add. The list shows matching surgeons, each with a round plus. Press a plus and the surgeon joins the campaign and appears at the bottom of the table. A surgeon already in the campaign has no plus. Press REPORT in the switch and the box goes back to being Search.
  • Not shown: the heading, the value chips and count tiles above this table, light mode, and what the plus does for a surgeon the person may not add (the existing rules apply).
