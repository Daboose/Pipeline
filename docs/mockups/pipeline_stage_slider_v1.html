Pipeline · Mockup: the stage slider for an implant opportunity (invented data, nothing built)

About this mockup

Is this what you meant? The stage path becomes the sliding one-pill switch from My Case Plan: the label STAGE on the white end, a shaded track holding all seven stages, and one highlight that slides to the stage the deal is at.

  1. Plan, Action, Commit, 1st Case, 5th Case: the highlight is blue (indigo) and the track stays its normal shade.
  2. Conversion: the whole pill turns green.
  3. Unsuccessful Conversion: the whole pill turns red.
  4. No stage set: every stage is shown on the plain track and nothing is highlighted.

Try it: the first row is live: click any stage and the highlight slides across (the colour changes with it). Below it, the same slider is shown once for each state, so you can judge the colouring of each. Dark and Light buttons are in the top bar.

Not shown: where it sits in the deal panel (it replaces the path in the top row), and the stage lists of risks and ROSA deals, which have their own stages. Earlier stages are not marked once the deal moves on, only the current one is highlighted; say if you want the passed stages marked.
