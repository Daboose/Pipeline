  • No tick: the slider as chosen in version 2, for comparison.
  • A. Plain tick: a small white tick right after the word.
  • B. Tick in a little circle: a small white circle with a tick in the slider's colour (blue, green or red) after the word.
  • Try it: click a stage on the big slider; the colour fades over 0.4 s, the white pill slides, and the ticks follow. All seven states are underneath. Person greyed, Theme dark.

The rule drawn: a stage gets a tick when the deal has moved past it, among the working stages (Plan to 5th Case). The current stage has none (it is the white pill), and Conversion never does (it is the end). When a deal is at Unsuccessful Conversion the working stages keep their ticks here; say if that should be different.
