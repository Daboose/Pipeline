PIPELINE MOCKUP: ONE PLACE FOR FILTERS (v1)

ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Robert asked for all filter handling to live in one panel opened from the top bar, instead of three places. Everything on this page is invented data; nothing is saved. The page behind (the top row, the options bar and the cards) is only a stand-in so you can see the panel in place.

WHAT CHANGES
  • The filter dropdown in the top row becomes a button showing the filter you are on (star if it is your default, and an amber CHANGED if what is on screen no longer matches it). Click it and the My filters panel flies down.
  • My filters in the cog (settings) panel goes: it would be a second place to manage them.
  • Save as filter in Change selection goes: saving now happens here, from whatever is on screen.
TRY IT
  • Click the filters button in the top row (it says ROBS1). The panel flies down, the page behind blurs; click the blurred page, the cross, or press Esc to close.
  • Click a filter's name to run it: the top bar and chips change and the panel folds away.
  • Star: makes it your default (only one can be; click the filled star to have no default). Pencil: rename in place (tick to keep, cross to cancel). Share icon: opens the share strip (a person, or your team if you manage one; sharing gives them their own copy). Bin: asks "Delete?" in the row first.
  • Click Pretend I changed the selection (the grey control above the page): the top button shows CHANGED, the active row too, and Save over "name" becomes available at the bottom. Press it and the filter takes what is on screen.
  • Save as new filter… asks for a name and saves what is on screen as a new filter, which becomes the one you are on.
  • The dark / light control and the full screen icon (top right of the page) work as in the other mockups.
WHERE FILTERS ARE KEPT (the answer to Robert's question)
  • In Pipeline's own object, Pipeline Saved Filter (Pipeline_Saved_Filter__c), not in COVRA's. One record per filter: the name, the choices as text (JSON), a default flag. It is private to its owner. Sharing creates a copy owned by the other person. Today UAT holds 2 test filters; PROD has none (nothing from Pipeline is in PROD yet).
CHOICES I MADE FOR YOU TO CHECK (not yet confirmed)
  • The panel drops from the top row on the right under the filters button, with the blurred page behind it, the same motion as Change selection (not a pop-up window).
  • Clicking a name runs the filter and closes the panel.
  • Save over is only available while a filter is active and the screen has changed from it; it says which filter it will overwrite, and there is no extra "are you sure".
  • Each row also shows a plain-words summary of what the filter holds (so two similar names can be told apart), and Share sits here as a fourth icon because sharing exists today and has to live somewhere.
  • Column choices still travel with a filter (as today).
NOT SHOWN YET
  • The Change selection panel and the cog panel without their filter parts; the filters on a phone.
