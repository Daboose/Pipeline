ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Proposal for a second mode on the Campaign view of Pipeline (reached by Change selection, pick a campaign, then CAMPAIGN at the top). Today the Campaign view is a report of what is linked. The new Maintain mode is for a manager to decide which deals are in the campaign. For each surgeon in the campaign it lists every open deal of theirs in the campaign's portfolio (Knee here), whoever owns it, with the brand, value, owner and stage, and a round + to put the deal in the campaign or a − to take it out. Report stays as it is (a surgeon with two linked deals takes two lines, as today), and is the mode the screen opens in. Invented data, shown at the new default Zoom of 80% (the cog opens the settings with the Zoom slider so you can try the other sizes). Ideas for the toggle's name are listed at the bottom of this panel.

  • Try it: press MAINTAIN beside Members, then press a + to add a deal. The deal turns green, says IN CAMPAIGN, and the tiles above change. Press the − to take it out again.
  • Try it: press REPORT to go back to the list of members you have today. Anything you added in Maintain shows there.
  • Try it: tick Risks and look at Daniel Tran's risk. It is owned by someone you cannot edit for, so its button is greyed (a person can only link a deal they can edit; that is the existing rule).
  • Try it: Carla Mendes and Frank Okafor have no open Knee deals, so there is nothing to add; they are still listed so the manager sees them.
  • Each press would save at once, with the "please wait" cover, as other changes do. There is no Save button.
  • Not shown: the real stage, rating and value columns of the report, search, the cog for columns and light mode. Mode names to consider: Report / Maintain (shown), Report / Add deals, View / Edit.
  • Try it: in Maintain, the two tick boxes left of the switch choose which kinds of deal are listed. Opportunities is ticked and Risks is not to begin with, because most of the time people add opportunities. Tick Risks to list risk deals too (Daniel Tran's risk appears); untick Opportunities and tick Risks to see only risks, for a risk campaign. ROSA deals are not offered at this stage. The tick boxes are only there in Maintain.
