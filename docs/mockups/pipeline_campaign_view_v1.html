ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. It shows the Campaign view Robert described on 2026-11-06: the extra screen Pipeline gets when a campaign is chosen in Change selection. The campaign is invented (Singapore Shoulder Symposium SG-MY-TH, Extremities, 50 surgeons); the real screen reads it from Salesforce.

  • Try the toggle: in the middle of the top bar press Pipeline and Campaign. The Pipeline screen slides off to the right and the Campaign screen comes in, and back again the other way. Only the row of Opportunities / Risks / Rosa / Open / Closed / Team stays still.
  • The Campaign screen: tiles at the top (surgeons, with a deal, no deal yet, deals, value per currency), then how the surgeon ratings split between those with and without a deal, where the deals are up to, and each country, then the list of every surgeon in the campaign with their deals.
  • Find the gaps: press No deal yet above the list. For a surgeon who is not in the campaign you can Remove (take the surgeon out of the campaign, with an Undo). Two of them already have a deal that was not linked: press Link it.
  • The Country column on the Pipeline screen: switch Campaign chosen to “No” below. The Country column disappears because the team is all in one country, and comes back when the selection spans several.
  • Click any deal row: in the real screen it opens the deal flyout (not drawn here). Light and dark both work.

My additions, for Robert to confirm: a Sales rep column (who to chase), the value shown per currency (SGD, MYR and THB are never added together), and the “Link it” shortcut. The first 20 surgeons are shown; “Show all” lists every one. The deal list on the Pipeline screen is simplified here.
