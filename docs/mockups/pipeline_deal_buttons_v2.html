ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 2: the two panel buttons move to the top, the bottom row is for actions only. Surgeon Usages and MyCase Bookings now sit beside the stage path at the top of the deal. The bottom row is one row that only ever holds the action buttons of the screen you are on, and is empty when there are none. Invented data only (Dr Example Surgeon).

What the bottom row holds (switch between Today and Proposed to compare):
  • The deal: Open in Salesforce on the bottom left; Cancel and Save changes on the bottom right (greyed until you change a field).
  • Surgeon Usages, just viewing: nothing.
  • Surgeon Usages, after Edit usages: Cancel and Save changes, bottom right only. After Add usage: Cancel and Add usage, bottom right only.
  • MyCase Bookings: nothing. Close a panel with its X, the blurred background, or by pressing its pill again.
Try it: the Jump to buttons show each state; or click the pills, Edit usages, Add usage and the X in the flyout; typing in a deal field switches on its Cancel and Save changes.

Not shown: the real fields of every deal type, Chatter posting, the slide-in motion. To check on a laptop screen: the two pills need about 460 pixels beside the path bar; on a narrower screen they would need to shrink (drop the badge) or wrap.
