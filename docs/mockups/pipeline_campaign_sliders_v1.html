About this mockup

Is this what you meant? It copies the look from My Case Plan (mycpSegmented and the filter bar mockup): a label on a white end, the shaded track, and one highlight that slides to the chosen side with a small spring and squash, all inside one outer pill.

  1. Theme (top right): the Dark and Light buttons become one slider with a sun (light) and a moon (dark) and the label THEME on the white end. Click it: the highlight slides across and the whole screen changes.
  2. Filters on the campaign list: Search, Manager, Country and Portfolio each get a label on a white end. The dropdowns are the coloured track itself. Show inactive becomes a slider: SHOW Active | All. Try them: pick "Me: Robert Reeves", a country, a portfolio, or type in the search box and use the round X.
  3. Scroll the list: the top bar and the column headings stay locked at the top (this part is already built in UAT as v0.23).

Not shown / decide: in dark mode the white label end takes the panel colour (as in My Case Plan's Navy). If you want the theme slider to carry words (LIGHT / DARK) as well as the icons, or the sun and moon without the THEME label, say so. If you like this, the same look would replace the other two-way choices on the form and the import screens too (for example the Active switch on the form).
