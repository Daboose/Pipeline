PIPELINE Pipeline Opportunities & Risks
ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. The subtle changes Robert listed, each shown as it is today (left) and as proposed (right) so they can be compared, before anything is changed.

  • 1. Home list: the deal's type on the second line. Today a card says “Commit · Q1”. Proposed: the portfolio first, “Knee · Commit · Q1” (Hip, Knee, Extremities or CMFT; for a risk its portfolio or portfolios; a ROSA deal keeps its deal type line as it is).
  • 2. Home list: the section headings 20% taller. Measured: a heading is 41 px high today; proposed 49 px (the same text, more room above and below). Charts, Opportunities, Risks and ROSA all change; open or folded.
  • 3. The New deal page. (a) More room between “Choose the kind, then fill it in.” and the Opportunity / Risk / ROSA slider: 10 px today, 16 px proposed. (b) The lines between the fields are taken out: between Primary HCP and Healthcare facility, Healthcare facility and the quarter, the quarter and the value, the value and the units; the line above Brand stays, as you said. (c) A couple of pixels more between each field's name and its box (5 px today, 7 px proposed). (d) The stage box folds: it starts open, because the stage is a choice you make when creating a deal (it starts at Plan, ticked), and tapping a stage folds it up to just “Stage” and the name you picked, like the stage row on a deal; tap the row to open it again. Today it never folds (try the left phone: nothing happens). (e) Fixing what I left out of the page's styling: the “‹ Back to My Pipeline” button is a plain blue word instead of the filled blue pill (shown as it is, on the left), the heading “New deal” is the browser's big default instead of the 24 px heading every other screen has, and the stage list lacks the fold that closes it (that is (d)). The proposed side shows them as every other phone screen has them.
  • 4. The same on editing an existing deal (the third pair; Robert, 2026-11-08: the two screens should look as if one person designed them). Where the fields are the same (the surgeon, the hospital, the quarter, the value, the units, the brand, the sliders, the link to ROSA) the proposed look is exactly the New deal page's: no lines between the first fields, the line above Brand, 7 px between a field's name and its box. The differences are only what must differ: the New deal page starts with the kind, the portfolio and the record type, and its stage box starts open; an existing deal has its stage as one folded row.
  • 5. How fast a screen slides (the first pair). Yes, it is ours to set. Today every slide takes 0.40 s; proposed 0.48 s, which is 20% slower. Press Slide both (or tap a card) and watch the two phones together; Back slides it off again. It would be one setting for the whole phone app (the screens, the search pages, the folds) and the desktop would stay as it is unless you say so.
  • 6. The saving screen (the second group). Pressing Save, Add or Create dims the whole screen, puts the Pipeline logo with “Saving…” on it and blocks every tap (so Back cannot be pressed by accident) until Salesforce has answered. It stays for at least a second so a quick save still feels like something happened. Then, depending on the switches above the phones: Saved ✓ first (a green tick for about 0.7 s, then back) or straight back; and if Salesforce refuses, the screen comes back at once with its message and nothing lost. It would be used for every save that is not an instant toggle: saving a deal, editing a usage, adding a usage, creating a deal, and deleting a usage (the second phone: press Delete this usage, answer the question, and the same screen says “Deleting…”; Robert, 2026-11-08: the operation must have happened before the screen comes back). The word on it is a choice: Saving… / Deleting… / Creating… each says what is happening, or one universal Updating… covers all of them; the switch above the phones shows both. Not for the instant ones (the Yes / No sliders, MyMobility, the campaign sliders, which save as you flip themthem). One limit: the Salesforce app's own bars (its title line, its top-left arrow and the tabs at the bottom) are outside our screen, so they stay undimmed and tappable; only our own Back bar and everything inside the page can be locked.
  • 7. The Brand component (the first group; Robert, 2026-11-08). It takes the stage control's look: the same indigo row and round chevron button. Closed, the row shows the chosen brands as plain words, exactly as the stage row shows the stage name: the brand, a comma, the next brand (or “None selected”); open, it unfolds to the brand chips to tap (selected ones indigo, as the chips are today), and folds shut again when you are done. An existing deal opens with it closed. It grows a little taller when several brands are chosen (the fourth phone, as on a risk), the names wrapping onto a second line. Height: measured, today the label and the ten chips of an opportunity take 175 px whether you want them or not; closed, the component is 64 px (about 110 px saved on every deal screen), a little more with several brands (81 px with four, the names running onto a second line) and 278 px while it is open. Tap the row or a chip on any of the four phones. The same component would be used on the New deal page and on editing a deal.
  • 8. State priority is gone from the phone (Robert, 2026-11-08: end users do not pick it). Every sliders block in this mockup now has only what an end user chooses: On hold and MyMo on an opportunity, Rep priority on all three kinds (a risk and a ROSA deal have no On hold or MyMo). It would come out of editing a deal and out of the New deal page; the desktop still has it for the managers who use it.
  • 9. MyMo is now MyMobility (Robert, 2026-11-08, the correct terminology). The slider reads MYMOBILITY (one word) and its stage field, which opens only while MyMobility is Yes, reads MyMobility stage. The sliders are now drawn with the new name. The slider labels all take the width of the longest one, so “MYMOBILITY” changes nothing about the sizes.
  • 10. The bar on the home screen is “Create New” (Robert, 2026-11-08: “deal” sounds like good news and a risk is not, and the bar only ever relates to the three things on that screen: Opportunities, Risks and ROSA). It has a circled plus on its left. The first group shows today's bar, the proposal, the same circled plus on the Usages screen's Add usage button so the two match (to confirm), and the page it opens, headed Create New to match (to confirm).
  • Try it: tap a stage on the right-hand New deal phone and it folds. The Show switch scrolls the two New deal phones to the same place, so the lines can be compared. Theme for dark.

Not shown: anything else. Nothing here changes how a deal is created or saved; no server code.
