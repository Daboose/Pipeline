ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

As you scroll down Pipeline, the Team, Opportunities, Risks and ROSA lists each keep their heading bar and their column names fixed under the top bar until you have scrolled past that list. The next list's bar then takes its place. Scrolling back up reverses it. The four lists use the real list styles; the data is invented.

  • Try it: scroll down slowly. The pink Team bar and its column names stay under the top bar, then Opportunities slides in and replaces them. Scroll back up and Team returns.
  • Try it: press the round arrow on a list's bar to fold it, then keep scrolling.
  • Try it: press "Make Opportunities wider than the screen" to see the one case where the column names do not stay (see below).
  • Known limit: a table made wider than the screen (many columns added) must scroll sideways inside its box, and a sideways-scrolling box cannot hold its column names still. In that case only the heading bar stays.
  • Not shown: the summary panels, the real top bar controls, and the phone (Pipeline Mobile is not affected).
