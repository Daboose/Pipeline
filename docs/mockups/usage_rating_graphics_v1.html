ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1: four ways to draw Platinum, Gold, Silver and Bronze. Option E (added after Robert liked the Adobe Stock medals) is drawn from scratch in that style, with the ribbon stripes counting the rank. The order is Platinum (highest), Gold, Silver, Bronze. Each family below draws all four, large, then at the size they would be on the usage card's rating tiles, then very small (as in a list or the deal flyout). All are drawn as vector graphics in the code, so there are no image files. The first row, "Today", is what the mockup of the card has now (a coloured dot). Invented data only.

Try it:
  • Switch Black and white on in the yellow controls: if you can still tell the four apart, the graphic does not rely on colour (for anyone who finds colours hard, and for printouts).
  • Look at the smallest size in each family: that is the test of a graphic.

Not shown yet: how it sits in the full card (that comes after you pick), "No rating" (a plain dashed outline circle, as in the card mockup), and any animation.
