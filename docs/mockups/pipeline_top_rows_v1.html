ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

The top of the campaign screen with the rows rearranged, at the 80% Zoom. Today the first row holds the campaign title on the left and the four count tiles on the right, and the Currency switch sits at the right of the value row underneath. Proposed: the two swap places. The first row becomes just the title on the left and the Currency switch on the right, and because neither is tall, its padding is reduced. The second row becomes all reporting chips: the Value / year chip and the five rating chips on the left and the four count tiles on the right, in Roboto Condensed Light with the number and the wording centred in each tile. The table moves up, so it gets more of the screen. Invented data.

  • Try it: the Layout switch flips between Today and Proposed, so you can see how much higher the table starts.
  • Try it: the Currencies in the data switch (mockup only) shows one, two or three currencies, so you can judge the second row with wide chips.
  • Try it: press the cog and change the Zoom to see how both rows hold.
Layout Currencies in the data These two switches are only for this mockup; they are not part of the product.
