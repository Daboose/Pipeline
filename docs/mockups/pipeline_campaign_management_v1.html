CAMPAIGN MANAGEMENT Campaigns Pipeline Accounts
ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

Version 1, invented data only. It shows how a campaign manager would set up a campaign, add members one at a time, and import a list with a check screen before anything is saved. It is the “Campaign Management” full-screen app Robert described on 2026-11-06. The Pipeline campaign screen (members view, graphs) and the Campaigns button on a deal are not in this mockup.

  • Try the whole flow: press New campaign, type a friendly name, pick countries (watch the name at the bottom grow its codes), pick a portfolio, press Save campaign. On the campaign press Import members, press Check names, then on the next screen tick the exact matches, pick the right person for “Tan Ah Kow”, look up “Jonathan Wu”, delete a row you do not want, and press Add members.
  • The name is never typed in. The person gives a friendly name (limited to 40 characters here) and the country codes are added after it, like Singapore Shoulder Symposium SG-MY-TH.
  • Only active campaigns can be linked to deals. Switch a campaign off with Deactivate and it drops out of the choices (and the list hides it until you show inactive ones).
  • Who can be a member: only real Salesforce physicians (person accounts of type physician) and emerging consultants. Staging accounts and clinic contacts are never offered. Type “wei” in Add member to see them held back.
  • Searching for surgeons: at least 2 characters are needed. It looks in the name and in the English name (the surgeon shown as 陈家豪 is found by typing “jia”). It stays inside the campaign’s countries until you press Widen search.
  • Use the Show buttons to jump to any screen. Light and dark both work.

Questions this mockup raises: is a campaign one portfolio or can it be several (shown as several here); the 40 character limit (assumed so the full name stays inside the 80 characters Salesforce allows); what extra details a campaign needs (a start and end date and a description are shown as examples). The app name is a placeholder. Hospitals are shown here as an example only: a surgeon can work at many hospitals, so the real screen would list every current hospital from the Account Relationship records, not one. The word is “Portfolio” (the events module’s “business segment”).
