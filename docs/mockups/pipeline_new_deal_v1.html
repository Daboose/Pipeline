PIPELINE Pipeline Sales Campaigns
ABOUT THIS MOCKUP

What it shows: the screen for a new deal, opened from the campaign list for a surgeon marked "No deal yet". It is the surgeon flyout (the one the rating plate opens) with a second pill, New Deal, beside Surgeon Usages. The form looks like a standard implant opportunity, everything editable. The record type is a choice (the portfolio, plus the country when the person works in more than one), the owner is the person using Pipeline and can be changed, the currency is the person's own and can be changed. Mandatory fields have a red *: the Primary HCP, the expected closing quarter and year (not in the past), the expected annual value and the expected annual units / cases (one box). Save stays off until they are filled. Invented data; the brand, owner and surgeon lists are typed in here (the real ones are read live from Salesforce).

Try it:
  • Press a Portfolio: the record type and the brand choices follow it. Switch Several countries to Yes above to see the Country list appear.
  • Open the Owner (press the round x) and pick someone else; open the surgeon (round x) to see the search.
  • Pick a quarter and a year in the past: the list for 2026 offers only the current quarter and later. Press the MyMobility Yes to see its stage.
  • Leave a mandatory box empty (Save stays grey), fill them in and press Save deal.
  • Under Opened by choose pressing the rating plate (opens on Surgeon Usages) or pressing "No deal yet" (opens on New Deal). Press Light or Dark.
Decided: a new deal made here is added to the campaign being looked at, and a deal can be created from the campaign only. Not shown yet: what the flyout does after Save (it could show the new deal's own screens, or close), a risk or a ROSA deal, the stage (a new deal starts at the first stage of its record type), the real lists, and the slide-in motion.
